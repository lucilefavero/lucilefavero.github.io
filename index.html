AI in education should scaffold human reasoning, not substitute for it. That is the argument of my research, stated in a position paper at the ACM AI Leadership Summit 2026, and the principle behind everything I build.

I am a doctoral researcher in the pan-European ELLIS PhD Program at ELLIS Alicante, supervised by Dr. Nuria Oliver (ELLIS Alicante), Prof. Tanja Käser (EPFL, ML4ED lab), and Prof. Juan Antonio Pérez-Ortiz (Universidad de Alicante). In my thesis, Socratic Dialogues for Learning: Toward AI-Enabled Human Active Learning, I develop Socratic educational chatbots that respond to students with critical questions rather than answers, fostering reflection, argumentative reasoning, and metacognition while limiting cognitive offloading. I test them where it counts: in real classrooms, with real students.

I was trained at the intersection of mathematics, computational neuroscience, and natural language processing, with an M.Sc. in Mathematics and an M.Sc. in Interdisciplinary Neuroscience from the University of Geneva, both with highest distinction. My long-term goal is to design AI systems that develop human capacities rather than automate them away.
